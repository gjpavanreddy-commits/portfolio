LABORATORY EXPERIENCE

Internships

CADD Intern — Indian Academy Degree College

Bengaluru · Mar – Apr 2025 · Computer-aided drug design (in silico)

An in-silico drug discovery internship at my own college, where I learned to take a candidate compound through preparation, docking and safety screening using a standard set of computational tools.

  • Docking: Docked candidate ligands into protein targets with AutoDock and PyRx, then compared binding poses and scores to identify which compounds fit the binding site best.
  • Preparation and analysis: Prepared ligand structures and analysed target proteins before docking, and examined the resulting protein–ligand interactions in Discovery Studio to understand how each compound binds.
  • ADMET: Screened candidate compounds for absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion properties and overall drug-likeness using SwissADME, to judge whether a compound could work as a drug.
  • Toxicity: Predicted the toxicity of candidate compounds with ProTox, so that molecules likely to be unsafe could be flagged early and removed from the shortlist.

AutoDock · PyRx · SwissADME · ProTox · Discovery Studio

Clinical Microbiology Intern — KIMS & Research Centre

Department of Microbiology, Bengaluru · Jun–Jul 2025 and Dec 2025–Jan 2026 · 212 hours in total, about 7.3 hours a day over 30 days

Two clinical stints in the Department of Microbiology, rotating through bacteriology, serology, immunology, virology, tuberculosis, mycology and parasitology, which gave me a working view of how a hospital diagnostic laboratory identifies infections.

  • Immunology and virology: ELISA for dengue NS1, dengue IgM/IgG and leprosy IgM; ECLIA on an automated platform; and rapid immunochromatographic tests for HIV, HBsAg and HCV.
  • Serology: Widal, Weil-Felix and VDRL/RPR tests, with interpretation of titres and an understanding of when a result needs a confirmatory assay.
  • Tuberculosis: Ziehl-Neelsen and Auramine O staining to detect acid-fast bacilli, along with microscopic examination of slit-skin smears.
  • Bacteriology: Media preparation and sterilization, culture and identification of sputum, blood and CSF samples, Gram staining, biochemical tests, and antibiotic sensitivity testing (Kirby-Bauer and VITEK 2), under aseptic and biosafety practice.

ELISA · ECLIA · Rapid tests · Widal · VDRL/RPR · ZN and Auramine O · Gram stain · VITEK 2

Summer Research Intern Fellow — Bio-Nanotechnology Laboratory

SRM University-AP, Amaravati · 25 Jun – 25 Jul 2026 · Advisor: Prof. Anil K Suresh

A one-month summer research fellowship in a bio-nanotechnology laboratory, covering nanomaterial synthesis, physicochemical characterization and a set of biochemical and biological assays in a working research setting.

  • Nanoparticle synthesis: Synthesized gold nanoparticles by green synthesis and by the Turkevich citrate-reduction method, and prepared silver nanoparticles, learning how reaction conditions shape the final particles.
  • Carbon dots: Prepared carbon dots by a hydrothermal method, a one-pot approach that uses heat and pressure in a sealed vessel to form fluorescent carbon nanoparticles.
  • Characterization: Characterized the materials by DLS for particle size, zeta potential for surface charge and stability, XRD for crystal structure, FTIR for functional groups and UV-Vis for optical properties.
  • Assays: Ran SDS-PAGE and Western blotting for protein analysis, plus antimicrobial, ABTS, TMB and 4-nitrophenol degradation assays to evaluate the biological and catalytic activity of the materials.

Green synthesis · Turkevich · Hydrothermal · DLS · Zeta potential · XRD · FTIR · UV-Vis · SDS-PAGE · Western blot
